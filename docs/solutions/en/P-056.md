# P-056. Proxy routing was configured, but the sing-box LaunchAgent was not loaded

[Русская версия](../P-056.md)

**Evidence status:** Partially verified by a 2026-09-23 maintenance receipt and a 2026-09-26 read-only audit. Startup was restored; the later route changed.

## Time and conditions
On 2026-09-23, Gemini web access on the iMac was repaired; on 2026-09-26, a separate read-only audit inspected a newer Google AI routing scheme.

## Symptoms
The local SOCKS listener was not listening even though PAC/proxy configuration existed.

## Observations vs actions
Codex/the operator separated startup failure from upstream/DNS issues: loaded the LaunchAgent, then separately updated upstream and DNS; on September 26 state was only read.

## Causal hypothesis
One confirmed cause was an unloaded sing-box LaunchAgent; not the only one, because upstream was stale and DNS needed correction.

## What would not have helped
Editing PAC/subscription without checking listener/process state was insufficient; the finding also should not be projected onto a future scheme.

## What helped
Checking listener/process state, loading the LaunchAgent, then checking upstream subscription and DNS without publishing credentials helped.

## Result check
On September 23, sing-box listened on loopback and Gemini `/app` returned HTTP 200 without an unsupported-country marker; later, the user confirmed Gemini/NotebookLM in the expanded scheme.

## Limits
By September 26, config and central routing had changed; the old startup fix does not prove an independent geo exit for every scenario.

## Basis
Basis: scoped Brain maintenance/lesson/canon records and git history in this documentation branch. Raw logs, private paths, domains/IPs, emails, secrets, and configurations are intentionally not published.
