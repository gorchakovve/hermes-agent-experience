# P-081. A panel password entered in the terminal could remain in shell history

**Status:** open by evidence; the available records do not prove that this exact risk was remediated.

[Русская версия](../P-081.md)

## Time and conditions

This belongs to the early OpenClaw/Hermes panel setup in April 2026. The public index records a handling risk: a panel password was entered through a terminal, so it could have remained in shell history, scrollback, terminal-session logs, or agent-side artifacts.

## Symptoms

This was not a login failure. It was a secret-handling risk. The dangerous sign is any command or argument where a credential is passed as plain text. The panel may keep working while the old value still remains visible to a later process or operator.

## Cause / hypothesis

The likely cause was choosing a fast CLI path instead of a secret prompt, vault flow, or restricted temporary credential file. For this specific case, no safe receipt was found proving that history was checked and the credential was rotated.

## Who did what

The public-safe evidence only shows that the risk was entered into the problem index. There is no separate record tying a dated actor to rotation, history cleanup, and a successful post-rotation login.

## What failed / what would help

Removing the password from an article is not enough: the risk lives in local histories and operational traces. A useful closure path would be: inspect traces without exposing the value, remove local history entries, rotate the password/token, verify login with the new value, and record a redacted receipt.

## Verification

No confirmed verification exists. To close the case, three facts are needed: the old credential is invalid, access works after rotation, and local history/log checks were completed without publishing the secret.

## Limits and evidence provenance

The basis is the public index plus indirect private operational notes. Raw shell history, logs, and the secret value are not public. Until a dedicated receipt exists, the status remains open.
