# P-084. The initial Hermes fallback to Gemini did not work as expected

**Status:** partially confirmed; OpenRouter was accepted as a practical fallback, but an end-to-end fallback to Gemini was not proven in the original event.

[Русская версия](../P-084.md)

## Time and conditions

On 27 April 2026, the Hermes fallback chain was checked. The primary provider was Codex; the configured chain then listed direct OpenAI, OpenRouter, and Gemini. The trigger was a primary-provider 429/usage limit.

## Symptoms

The configured expectation did not match observation. When the primary provider hit 429, Hermes did not show an observed attempt through the direct OpenAI provider and moved straight to OpenRouter. Gemini was not reached in that event and therefore did not prove that it worked as the final emergency fallback.

## Cause / hypothesis

The issue was not simply “Gemini is broken.” It was a mismatch between declared fallback order and runtime behavior. Configuration, actual routing, and observed logs must be verified separately. If an intermediate fallback succeeds, the later fallback can remain untested.

## Who did what

The operator/agent recorded the live config and observed behavior under 429. The decision was not to churn a working config immediately: OpenRouter already provided a practical reserve, while Gemini stayed as the final emergency option. Later audits confirmed that Google/Gemini routes require a proxy and must not be removed blindly.

## What failed / what helped

It failed to treat “Gemini is present in config” as proof of failover. What helped was separating three claims: OpenRouter worked, direct OpenAI was not observed in the checked event, and Gemini was not tested in that event.

## Verification

Confirmed result for the original case: practical fallback to OpenRouter was accepted as working state. Not confirmed: a controlled smoke where the primary and intermediate fallbacks are unavailable and Hermes successfully reaches Gemini and answers.

## Limits and evidence provenance

The basis is a private operational record from 27 April 2026 plus later notes about the Google/Gemini proxy route. Raw logs and configs are not public. This case must not claim that Gemini was restored by the original action.
