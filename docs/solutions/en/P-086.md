# P-086. OpenClaw entered a `pairing required` loop and did not answer in Telegram

**Status:** open by evidence; a Telegram allowlist runbook was found, but no receipt closes the exact pairing loop.

[Русская версия](../P-086.md)

## Time and conditions

The problem appears in the index near the June Telegram/OpenClaw work. The dated record found for 15 June describes a Telegram group allowlist procedure and visible replies, but it does not contain the original `pairing required` log.

## Symptoms

The user expects a Telegram reply, but OpenClaw does not answer and ends up in `pairing required` instead of a normal conversation. Different causes can look similar: disallowed chat, disallowed user, unpaired device, wrong topic policy, or a silent `NO_REPLY` pattern.

## Cause / hypothesis

The available records clearly separate Telegram allowlist from device/account pairing. Allowlist determines where the bot may answer; pairing determines whether the device/channel is bound. Fixing allowlist can restore group replies, but it does not prove that a pairing loop was fixed.

## Who did what

A runbook was written: Telegram remains in allowlist mode; a new group is added through a helper; the helper backs up config, adds the allowed group, configures visible replies, and restarts the gateway. A separate guard prevents a final silent `NO_REPLY` where a visible reply is required.

## What failed / what helped

It failed to lump all Telegram non-replies into one class. If the cause is pairing, adding a group to allowlist may not be sufficient. The useful diagnostic sequence is layered: chat/user allowlist, group/topic policy, device/account pairing state, gateway health, then a control reply in the same Telegram context.

## Verification

Confirmed: a runbook exists and defines expected behavior for allowed groups. Not found: the original `pairing required` log, the pairing action, and a before/after test where the same Telegram context started answering.

## Limits and evidence provenance

The basis is private runbook/maintenance records for Telegram allowlist and visible-reply policy. Raw Telegram IDs, configs, and logs are not public. The case remains open until there is a receipt with a specific pairing state and visible reply.
