# P-031. Two Google Drive accounts made the authorization plan ambiguous

[Русский](../P-031.md) · [Index](../../problem-index.en.md)

**Status:** verified for folder creation through the helper on 24 May 2026; not proof of full Drive/Docs coverage.

## Conditions and symptoms

During Google Workspace/Drive setup, agents had access to two separate Drive contexts, but the direct connector did not expose a simple “create folder” action. The initial plan mixed two routes: a local desktop sync tree and cloud folder creation through a VPS helper. The practical risk was creating content in the wrong account, or assuming the capability was unavailable when only one connector operation was missing.

## Cause and testable hypothesis

The evidence supports this hypothesis: Drive access was not wholly absent; account selection and a specific connector limitation were the issue. The local path depends on the intended account being visible in the sync tree. The cloud path needs a helper with an explicit account parameter.

Generic safe example:

```sh
gdrive-create-folder --account personal --name "Smoke folder"
gdrive-create-folder --account business --name "Smoke folder" --parent "https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/<folder_id>"
```

Do not rely on a default account when more than one Drive is connected.

## What helped and what did not prove success

What helped was separating the routes: local sync for already visible trees, and a cloud helper for folder creation. What did not help was treating the absence of one connector operation as evidence that the whole capability was unavailable.

Attribution: this is based on the team's operational receipt, not on a fresh reproduction while writing this article.

## Validation and limits

The 24 May record says the helper created and immediately trashed smoke-test folders in both accounts. A separate audit from the same date recorded successful two-account Workspace MCP checks. Limit: this validates account choice and folder creation, not every Drive/Docs permission.
