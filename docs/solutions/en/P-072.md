# P-072. Kaspersky TLS inspection broke Grok Bot on Lenovo

**Status:** cause confirmed for the temporary workaround; the permanent narrow exception with protection re-enabled still needed verification.

**Русская версия:** [P-072](../P-072.md)

## Timeframe and conditions
2026-09-27. Lenovo laptop, VPN connected, Grok Bot 0.61.0. The VPN was an operating requirement and was not disabled as a diagnostic experiment.

## Symptoms
Grok Bot showed connection errors while ordinary HTTPS and VPN traffic worked. That made VPN, subscription status or the app installer tempting but unproven explanations.

## Confirmed cause vs hypothesis
Confirmed: a TLS probe for the affected Cursor/Grok-related host showed a certificate issued by a local Kaspersky root. After HTTPS inspection was temporarily disabled, the same host showed a public CA chain, and after relaunch Grok Bot displayed the bot list, an existing conversation and the composer without a connection error.

Not confirmed: VPN failure, SuperGrok entitlement failure, installer failure or account failure.

## Actor attribution and actions
Codex/Lenovo diagnostics proposed comparing the TLS issuer before and after the controlled change. The user temporarily disabled Kaspersky HTTPS inspection. After app relaunch, the user confirmed Grok Bot worked. VPN stayed connected.

What must not be preserved as the “solution”: leaving antivirus protection fully disabled. That was a diagnostic bypass, not a permanent configuration.

## Practical verification
First compare the certificate issuer for the affected host:

```sh
openssl s_client -connect <host>:443 -servername <host> </dev/null 2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -issuer -subject
```

Then relaunch the app and check the UI: bot list, existing chat, composer, no connection error. For a lasting fix, add a narrow encrypted-traffic-scanning exception for the required app/domains, re-enable protection and repeat the TLS plus UI smoke.

## Proof of result
The record confirms that before the change the certificate was intercepted by a Kaspersky root; after temporary disablement the chain became public; Grok Bot opened the bot list, chat and composer; the user explicitly confirmed it worked; VPN was not disconnected.

## Limits and open items
The permanent Kaspersky exception with protection re-enabled was not confirmed. If it recurs, do not blame VPN automatically; repeat the TLS issuer check and the full app restart smoke.

## Lesson
When one Electron/desktop app cannot connect while ordinary HTTPS works, check local TLS inspection. Changing VPN without comparing certificates may fix the wrong thing.
