# P-068. Old and new VPN containers confused active profiles

[Русский](../P-068.md) · [Index](../../problem-index.en.md)

**Status:** partially confirmed lifecycle analysis. By the preflight date the old container was already gone; the exact actor/timing of removal between stages is not established.

## When and conditions
Migration on 2026-09-18 and a later read-only preflight on 2026-09-26.

## Symptoms
During the AWG 2 → AWG 3.1 transition, both the old rollback path and the new working path appeared in notes. Later records could make it look as if the old container still needed deletion, or as if a similarly named image was garbage.

## Cause vs. hypothesis
As of 2026-09-26, the old AWG2 container was no longer present. At the same time, one similarly named image was an ancestor of the active AWG3.1 container. Therefore “old-looking name means delete it” was the wrong hypothesis.

## What helped
A read-only inventory checked live containers, stopped containers, image ancestry, module/tools, and Google AI services without deleting anything. The conclusion: do not recreate AWG2 from old notes, and do not delete an ancestor/rollback artifact without proof.

## Verification
The active AWG3.1 path and Google AI routing were working; post-discovery probes responded. No containers, images, peers, rollback artifacts, or profiles were deleted during the preflight.

## What is not proven
The exact intermediate moment and responsible actor for AWG2 removal conflict across available records. Cosmetic cleanup is not considered completed and should not be claimed retroactively.
