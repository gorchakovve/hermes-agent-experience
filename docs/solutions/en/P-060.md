# P-060. NotebookLM and avatar hosts were outside the selective Google AI route

[Русская версия](../P-060.md)

**Evidence status:** Verified by a 2026-09-24 receipt and user confirmation. The avatar cause was not proven by single HEAD checks; the final result was confirmed by the user.

## Time and conditions
2026-09-24, after browser Gemini was restored. NotebookLM web app and avatar had to be checked in the user’s signed-in Chrome profile, which the agent could not access.

## Symptoms
NotebookLM redirects went to new hostnames outside the rules; the avatar did not appear. The native Gemini app was arm64-only on Intel and could not solve it.

## Observations vs actions
The operator did not read the signed-in profile; redirect chain/rules were checked externally, a narrow host addition was proposed, the operator explicitly allowed it, and only local sing-box was restarted.

## Causal hypothesis
NotebookLM failure is best explained by redirects to hostnames outside the selective route; for the avatar, single HEAD checks proved only reachability, not the exact image cause.

## What would not have helped
A broad user-content rule, account changes, cookie extraction, VPS/VPN changes, or an arm64 native app on Intel were unnecessary/unsafe/impossible.

## What helped
Adding only exact NotebookLM redirect/login/avatar hosts to PAC/sing-box, preserving default route, and checking config/listeners/redirect chain helped.

## Result check
The redirect reached login without loop/error; the control browser reached sign-in; the operator confirmed NotebookLM in the Chrome web app and the avatar appeared.

## Limits
The repair did not fix VPS IP regional classification and did not prove subscription lifetime; it verifies the narrow iMac browser route on the check date.
