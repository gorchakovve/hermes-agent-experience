# P-070. The German VPS did not open NotebookLM

[Русский](../P-070.md) · [Index](../../problem-index.en.md)

**Status:** open; confirmed regional failure for NotebookLM through the direct VPS path; a full fix through a new owned IP/IPv6 was not implemented.

## When and conditions
2026-09-24, iMac and the direct path through the existing Amnezia/VPS setup. The check was compared with the working selective Polish Google AI route.

## Symptoms
NotebookLM through the direct VPS path redirected to `location=unsupported`. Through the selective Polish route, the same service reached Google login. Gemini’s public HTML could return HTTP 200, but that did not prove a working signed-in chat.

## Cause vs. hypothesis
The strong hypothesis is Google-side regional classification of the IP: external trace showed a European exit, while the Gemini interface described the location as Moscow/Russia. This supports a Google-service classification problem, but does not prove the provider has fixed the address or that a new IP would solve it.

## What helped
For diagnosis, only Google AI was temporarily sent through the direct path, then the known-good PAC with the selective route was restored byte-for-byte. VPN, VPS, keys, and firewall were not changed. Later, after explicit user permission, an informational request was sent to the hosting provider; no paid service or settings change was made.

## Verification
The NotebookLM refusal reproduced both from the iMac direct path and directly from the VPS without a browser or account. After restoring the selective route, Gemini returned a real short answer and the PAC restoration was checked. The hosting control panel was a separate case: see [P-062](P-062.md).

## What is not proven
No additional IPv4/IPv6 was ordered or tested. A new address cannot be promised to fix Google’s refusal. Gemini HTTP 200 through the German path is not equivalent to a successful signed-in chat.
