# P-055. An autossh monitor port was mistaken for a broken reverse SSH route

[Русская версия](../P-055.md)

**Evidence status:** Partially verified. There is an operational lesson and an archive pointer to the maintenance receipt; this smoke did not prove long-term reverse-route stability or the separate fail2ban context.

## Time and conditions
2026-05-31. iMac↔VPS reverse access was used by agents for diagnostics; nearby lessons covered autossh throttling/fail2ban.

## Symptoms
`remote port forwarding failed` was interpreted as a broken VPS→iMac SSH endpoint, but the message referred to the autossh monitor port.

## Observations vs actions
The operator separated direct VPS SSH, the real reverse listener, and the monitor port; backed up the LaunchAgent, switched autossh to `-M 0`, kept SSH keepalives, and restarted only the tunnel agent.

## Causal hypothesis
The failure was monitor-port noise; the main reverse route worked at check time.

## What would not have helped
It was wrong to treat the monitor port as the endpoint, change the main SSH route, or investigate fail2ban without a proven main-path failure.

## What helped
Identifying the service listener versus monitor listener and removing the monitor port for this LaunchAgent helped.

## Result check
The required reverse listener remained; the monitor listener disappeared; a test reverse SSH returned expected user/host/cwd; related services were active.

## Limits
Indefinite tunnel stability was not proven; apply fail2ban lessons only after checking its involvement.
